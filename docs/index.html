Personal writing here.

Hello

If you reach here, you have found my personal blog. I write something personal here, under this domain. This is the place I write something with my personal touch, I have a main website, it also has a blog section, mostly I put some technical notes and articles there. I started this blog subdomain because at this point, I feel writing could be a good way for self exploration, and I also have a feeling that as an overthinker, I have many thoughts everyday, I want to keep a record. So writings here might not be serious at all, or it might get very serious.

This site is generated with oxie, a small Python toolkit I wrote. Anyone's welcome to use it.

如果你来到了这里，那么你已经找到了我的个人博客。我会在这个域名下写一些更私人的东西：https://blog.puyuwang.org/。我也有一个主网站：https://puyuwang.org。那里同样有一个博客版块，不过我主要会放一些技术笔记和文章。之所以开始这个独立的博客子域名，是因为到了现在这个阶段，我越来越觉得，写作或许是一种很好的自我探索方式。同时，作为一个很容易想太多的人，我每天脑子里都会冒出很多不同的想法，而我想把它们记录下来。所以，这个博客里的内容可能不会那么严肃——也可能会非常严肃。

另外， 我这个网站是用 oxie 生成的。这个工具包是我自己做的，但是也欢迎任何人使用。
