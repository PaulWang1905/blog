# Garlic Butter Prawns

## Ingredients

- 50g butter
- Raw Prawns (peeled)
- Chopped Garlic (3 or 4 cloves)
- Mixed Herbs (might contain Thyme, Basil, Parsley...)
- Salt and Pepper
- Rosemary (we wanted to use this but didn't have any at the time, so we used mixed herbs instead)
- Chopped Onion (1)
- Starch powder



## Pipeline

1. **Marinate:** Coat the prawns with starch powder and pepper, then marinate for 10 minutes.
2. **Fry:** Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat. Once melted, add the chopped garlic and cook for 1 minute. Then add the chopped onion. Cook for 1 or 2 minute. 
3. **Cook the prawns:** Add the raw prawns and fry until they turn pink/red. Add the mixed herbs and salt (and rosemary, if available), and stir well. Cook for 5 - 7 minutes in total. 


I would definitely try again when I have rosemary.
