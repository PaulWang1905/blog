Recently I made a purchase of a Windows Phone -- a Nokia Lumia 635. 
It says time since last charge: 4313 days 18 hours. That is 11 years, 9 months, and 28 days. 

I owned two windows phone before: a Huawei W1 and Lumia 1320. None of them worked perfectly. Huawei never bothered to support W1 as Nokia would and I struggled using it. I still remember reading articles about how new features are getting released and I did not get the chance to use it. Around 2014, when I was going to undergraduate study in Zhengzhou for musicology (music and dance), I switched to Nokia - Lumia 1320. I thought my struggle for updates would end and I was actually happy about it  for a couple of years, until they had windows 10 mobile, and my lumia could not get the update. Somehow I could not use WeChat either. Nevertheless, I remember I had a great time using windows phone: Nokia Camera, MixRadio, etc. I still like its design and the live tiles. But around 2015, I realised that Lumia is not a real phone anymore. It is not about it's been abandoned by MS later.
